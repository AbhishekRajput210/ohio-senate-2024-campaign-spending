# Question 1: Campaign Spending Trends

**Metric:** total spent = sum of operating expenditures by payment date, for the campaign committees.

**Time periods:** 2023; Jan 1 to Mar 19 2024 (before the primary); Mar 20 to Aug 31; Sep 1 to Nov 5 (final 66 days); after Election Day.

## Findings
- Brown's campaign spent $97.46M and Moreno's $25.82M (3.8 times).
- Brown's lead was $4.4M at the end of 2023, $10.2M at the primary and $70.3M on Election Day.
- Both campaigns peaked in October 2024 (Brown $19.16M, Moreno $6.30M).
- Moreno spent earlier: 20.3% of his money was paid in 2023 against 9.9% for Brown. About 39% of each campaign's money was spent in the final 66 days.
- Voter communication was 81% to 91% of Brown's spending in each 2024 period before Election Day, and 72% to 90% for Moreno.
- A few very large payments matter: Brown made 30 payments over $1M (44.8% of his spending) and Moreno made 6 (31.1%).

## Limits
Payment dates are not airing dates, and the results do not show that an event caused a change in spending.
