# Question 3 (Extension): Outside Groups

**Question:** did outside spending offset Brown's campaign spending advantage?

## Findings
- Outside groups spent $286.67M, about 70% of all spending in the race.
- Spending that helps Moreno was $177.52M and spending that helps Brown was $109.15M. The net $68.37M for Moreno is about 95% of Brown's $71.64M campaign lead.
- Campaign plus outside spending was $206.61M for Brown's side and $203.34M for Moreno's, so Brown's side ended $3.3M ahead.
- Moreno's side was ahead from 24 Sep to 30 Oct 2024 (36 days).

- The results show how spending lined up, not what caused votes.
