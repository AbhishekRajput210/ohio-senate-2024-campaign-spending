# Question 2: Campaign Comparison

**Scope:** campaign committees only. Affiliated committees appear only in the committee chart. Outside groups are in Question 3.

## Findings
- Brown spent $97.46M and Moreno $25.82M, a gap of $71.64M. Brown made 2,517 payments and Moreno 2,183, so the gap comes from bigger payments.
- TV, radio and other media (+$31.84M) and digital advertising (+$22.42M) explain about 76% of the gap.
- Both campaigns spent about 80% on voter communication (Brown 82.3%, Moreno 79.7%). Digital was 25% of Brown's budget and 7% of Moreno's.
- Each campaign relied on one media vendor: Grassroots Media got 48.1% of Brown's spending and Strategic Media Placement got 62.2% of Moreno's.
- Moreno spent more on communication in 2023 Q4 ($3.18M against $0.81M). Brown spent far more in 2024 Q2 and Q3.
